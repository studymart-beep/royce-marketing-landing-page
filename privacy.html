Who we are

R0YCE Marketing ("we", "us") is a marketing agency offering Meta Ads and TikTok Ads management, advertising creative production, landing page design and campaign optimisation. You can reach us at roycemarketing72@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at 0904 243 6206.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details you submit: your name, email address, business name, monthly advertising budget, the service you're interested in, and any message you choose to write.
  • Messages you send us directly by email or WhatsApp.
  • Advertising and analytics signals described in the next section — these do not include your name, email or phone number.

Cookies, pixels and analytics

This website uses the Meta Pixel (Facebook) to measure page views and the performance of our advertising. It may also load a TikTok Pixel if one is configured. These tools set cookies and may recognise your browser across sites, and they are operated by Meta and TikTok under their own privacy policies.

We record events such as a page view, a view of our services content, a click on WhatsApp or email, and a successfully submitted enquiry. We do not send your name, email address, phone number or message contents to these platforms.

You can limit this tracking with your browser's privacy settings, an ad blocker, or the platform's own ad settings.

How enquiries are delivered

The enquiry form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards the details to our business inbox. FormSubmit handles the data only to deliver the message to us.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a strategy call.
  • To prepare a proposal or plan for your business.
  • To improve this website and understand which of our pages and ads are useful.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not add you to a marketing list without your agreement.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up, and are deleted on request.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing roycemarketing72@gmail.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

Changes
