FAQ

Common questions

Which advertising platforms do you manage?

Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok Ads. We don't offer Google or YouTube advertising management, so if that's what you need we'd rather point you elsewhere than take the work.

Is the ad budget included in the monthly price?

No. The monthly fee covers management and production. Your advertising spend is paid separately, directly to Meta and TikTok from your own ad accounts — which stay in your name.

How soon do campaigns go live?

The first creative batch and campaign build are in week one, with tracking installed alongside. Most accounts have enough data for a first real read within 30–45 days.

What budget should I start with?

A workable starting point is around $1,000–$2,000 per month in media spend so a test can reach a decision. Below that we usually suggest starting with creative production while you build the library.

Who owns the ad accounts and the creative?

You do. Ad accounts, pixel, dashboards and every creative file we produce stay yours, including after we stop working together.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise — nobody can guarantee ad performance. What we commit to is a written plan with a target number, a weekly test read you can check, and an honest conversation if the account isn't moving.

How do we communicate during a project?

A shared Slack or WhatsApp channel, a weekly written update, and one live call per month. You should never have to ask what's happening in your account.
