FAQ

Common questions.

What exactly is "AI UGC"?

Creator-style video produced with AI avatars, voices and editing tools instead of booking a creator for every shoot. It keeps the native, phone-shot feel that performs in paid social, while letting us produce more hook and angle variants per month at lower cost and turnaround.

Do you only work with coaches and digital products?

That's our focus, but we also work with service businesses and ecommerce brands where the numbers make sense. The strategy call is the honest filter — if we don't think we can help, we'll say so.

How soon do campaigns go live?

First creative batch and campaign build land in week one, with the pixel and events installed alongside. Most accounts have enough data for a first real read within 30–45 days.

What budget do I need?

A workable starting point is around $1,000–$2,000 per month in media spend, so tests can reach a decision. Below that we usually recommend starting with Creative Only while you build the creative library.

Do I own the creative and the accounts?

Yes. Assets, scripts and raw files are yours to keep, including after we stop working together. The same goes for your ad accounts, pixel and dashboards.

Do you guarantee results?

No agency can guarantee ad performance. What we do commit to is a written plan with a target number, a weekly test read you can check, and an honest conversation if the account isn't moving in the right direction.

How do we communicate?

A shared Slack or WhatsApp channel, a weekly written update, and one live call per month. You should never have to ask what's happening in your account.
